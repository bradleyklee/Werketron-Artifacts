# Beads knowledge-creation results

The research was represented in Beads and read back successfully. The recorded
findings distinguish database queries from history synchronization, Beads
relationships from network routing, selected compare-and-swap behavior from
whole-resource revision guarantees, and BDP demonstrations from complete
served profiles. A further accepted synthesis compares Beads’ native HTTP
backend with BDP; the chapter gives the provisional recommendation and its
revisit conditions.

The evidence is linked from the [chapter](Beads-on-the-Server.md), the
[claim coverage record](source-link-coverage.md), and the pinned
[source studies](source-reports/). No semantic extraction, inference, or graph
consolidation was performed. These results record bounded research and
read-back checks; they do not claim that the software inferred new knowledge.
