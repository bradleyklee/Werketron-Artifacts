# Pilot direction

Create an Italian Fibonacci product through the normal STARCHART process and
use the opportunity to establish a public product-plus-receipts form. Compare
current Luna and Sol workers using the same frozen specification, retain their
transaction accounting when available, and avoid inventing monetary costs.

The export must contain the prompt, raw Bead prints, notes describing the
process, raw relevant transaction records, and the final candidate products.
It must be easy for an adversarial AI reviewer to inventory, reproduce, and
challenge the claimed Fibonacci relationships.

This file is a scoped durable transcription of the pilot direction used to
form `SPEC.md` and `PLAN.json`; it is not represented as a byte-for-byte export
of the conversational transcript.
