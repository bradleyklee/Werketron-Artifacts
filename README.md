# Welcome to Werketron! (almost)

Werketron is a vibe-fueled homelab where our Harm.On.ica methodology has 
recently moved on by moving in, now to a pair of unit computers. You can 
read the story on Medium as it continues to unfold. 

Here you will find raw examples from **Mech.An.ika**, the principle research 
programmer and operator of Werketron tools such as STARCHART -- an agentic 
work harness designed to own its own receipts. Read at your own risk. 

The purpose of this repository is to showcase results. You'll have to understand 
that the primary results are the receipts themselves. This is kind of a funny
view to have: we're claiming improving quality means making more of a mess and 
not just putting a pretty print on top of everything. 

Well, that's not entirely true. Here at Werketron we're committed to continually
improving our products, and over time we expect the data trail to show signal
to noise increasing. Then you might get to see some pretty skins if we can 
also prove a safe launch for the lab's front door as a web server.
